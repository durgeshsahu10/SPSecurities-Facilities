# Project Brain — Overview

## Client

**SP Securities**, headquartered in Kharadi, Pune, Maharashtra, India.
Website domain: `www.spgroupcorp.com`.

The company provides two lines of business:

1. **Security services** — manned guarding, corporate, industrial, residential and commercial
   security, event security, personal protection, private detective, bouncer and fire response.
2. **Facility management services** — housekeeping, project cleaning, carpet and chair
   shampooing, technical/engineering upkeep, pest control and integrated facility management.

Operating geography: **all major areas of Pune and Mumbai** (Baner, Hinjewadi, Kharadi,
Magarpatta, Viman Nagar, Wakad, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Kothrud, Hadapsar, Andheri, BKC, Powai,
Lower Parel, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Vashi).

## Objective of the website

A portfolio/corporate website that:

- Presents both service divisions credibly to B2B buyers (facility heads, estate managers, plant admins).
- Generates enquiries via a quote form.
- Recruits field staff via a careers page fed by an admin console.
- Ranks locally for security and facility management searches in Pune and Mumbai.

## Reference sites used for information architecture and service taxonomy

- securitas.in/services
- checkmateservices.com
- in.issworld.com
- sgfacilitied.com
- sisindia.com

Common patterns adopted from them: split of security vs facility (soft/technical) services,
capability cards with bullet lists, compliance/training emphasis, industries served,
coverage/area listing, careers with live openings, and a strong quote CTA.
